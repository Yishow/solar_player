## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Align circuit card height to settings page footer

The system SHALL set the height of the circuit settings card (`cs-card`) to 740px to align with other backend settings surfaces and fill the layout down to the footer's top edge.

#### Scenario: Circuit card height is aligned

- **WHEN** the circuit settings page is rendered on the FHD canvas
- **THEN** the height of the `.cs-card` container SHALL be 740px
